Set it up

  1. Put Axiom.exe in its own folder, such as one on your Desktop. Your macros and settings are saved next to it.
  2. Double-click it. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. If your antivirus blocks it, add the folder as an exception. Tools that send keyboard and mouse input are often flagged by mistake.
  4. Open Roblox, pick the Missions or Raids page in Axiom, and press F1 to record your first macro.
